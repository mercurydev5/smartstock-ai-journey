## 🌍 English Must-Follow Channels

### 🔬 Research & Deep Dives

#### 1. Andrej Karpathy ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy
- **Content:** Building neural networks from scratch, GPT, LLMs
- **Must-watch:**
  - "Let's Build GPT" (2.5 hours - legendary!)
  - "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" series
  - "State of GPT"
- **Why follow:** Former OpenAI Director, clearest LLM explanations ever

#### 2. 3Blue1Brown ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown
- **Content:** Mathematical intuitions with beautiful animations
- **Must-watch:**
  - Neural Networks series (4 videos)
  - Linear Algebra series (15 videos)
  - Calculus series
- **Why follow:** Math never felt this beautiful before

#### 3. Yannic Kilcher ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@YannicKilcher
- **Content:** AI paper readings and discussions
- **Must-watch:**
  - "Attention Is All You Need" walkthrough
  - GPT-3, BERT paper reads
  - AI controversies
- **Why follow:** Best paper explanation videos on YouTube

#### 4. Two Minute Papers ⭐⭐

## 🚫 What NOT to Watch

- ❌ Channels that promise "Master ML in 7 days"
- ❌ Clickbait titles: "I made $10K with AI overnight"
- ❌ Outdated tutorials (check the upload date!)
- ❌ Too many channels simultaneously (pick 3-4 max)
