## 🌍 Global Communities

### Online Forums & Discussions

#### 1. r/MachineLearning (Reddit) ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://reddit.com/r/MachineLearning
- **Size:** 3M+ members
- **Best for:** Latest AI research, paper discussions, news
- **Tip:** Follow the weekly discussion threads

#### 2. Hugging Face Discord ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/join/discord
- **Best for:** LLMs, transformers, model sharing
- **Key channels:** #general, #nlp, #model-training, #paper-discussion

#### 3. fast.ai Forums ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://forums.fast.ai
- **Best for:** Practical deep learning questions
- **Culture:** Very beginner-friendly and helpful

#### 4. Kaggle Community ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/discussion
- **Best for:** Competition discussions, datasets, notebooks
- **Tip:** Follow top grandmasters

#### 5. Papers with Code ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://paperswithcode.com
- **Best for:** Finding implementations of ML papers
- **Feature:** State-of-the-art benchmarks

#### 6. AI Stack Exchange ⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://ai.stackexchange.com
- **Best for:** Technical Q&A
- **Note:** More formal than Reddit

#### 7. ML Twitter/X Community ⭐⭐⭐
- **Best accounts to follow:**
  - @karpathy (Andrej Karpathy)
  - @ylecun (Yann LeCun)
  - @GaryMarcus (Gary Marcus - AI critic)
  - @emollick (Ethan Mollick)
  - @hardmaru (David Ha)
  - @jeremyphoward (Jeremy Howard, fast.ai)

#### 8. LinkedIn AI Community ⭐⭐⭐
- Best for building in public
- Follow: Andrew Ng, Yann LeCun, Fei-Fei Li
- Post your projects here! (Indian audience = better engagement)

## 📅 How to Use Communities Effectively

### Daily (5 minutes):
- Check Twitter/X ML feed
- Scan r/MachineLearning top posts

### Weekly (30 minutes):
- Participate in one forum discussion
- Share your project/progress on LinkedIn

### Monthly:
- Attend one virtual meetup/webinar
- Submit one project to Kaggle or HF Spaces

### Build Your Reputation:
```
1. Answer questions on Stack Exchange / Reddit
2. Share Bengali ML tutorials (rare = valuable!)
3. Contribute to AI4Bharat
4. Write Bengali AI blog posts
5. Create your own content about the journey
```
